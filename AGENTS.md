# AGENTS.md

## Project Positioning

`Interactive Trainings` is a focused B2B service, not a generic SaaS platform and not an "all-in-one" training product.

The offer is narrow and concrete:
- We convert a company's existing SOPs, PDFs, slide decks, and videos into short interactive safety and compliance training modules.
- The target learners are multilingual frontline teams in the EU.
- The landing page must sell a specific operational result and a pilot offer, not an abstract product vision.

## Target Buyers

Primary buyers:
- Operations leaders
- Safety / compliance managers
- L&D buyers

Priority industries:
- Warehousing
- Logistics
- Field service
- Manufacturing

## Core Message

The landing page should emphasize:
- Training that works on the floor
- Mobile-ready delivery
- Multilingual training for frontline teams
- SCORM-ready output
- Compatibility with existing LMS setups
- No LMS replacement

## Tone And Writing Rules

Use a tone that is:
- Practical
- Clear
- Operational
- Enterprise-safe

Avoid:
- Product fluff
- Generic SaaS language
- "Platform for everything" framing
- Generic marketing cliches
- Unverified claims about performance uplift, ROI, completion improvement, incident reduction, or similar outcomes

## Messaging Guardrails

When information is missing, prefer:
- Simple and convincing B2B-plausible messaging
- Concrete operational benefits
- Claims that can be defended without invented data

Do not invent:
- Customer proof
- Metrics
- Case-study outcomes
- Compliance guarantees
- Enterprise capabilities that have not been explicitly defined

## Landing Page Intent

The landing page should sell:
- A clear transformation from existing training materials into short interactive modules
- A low-friction pilot offer
- Operational fit for frontline environments

The landing page should not sell:
- A broad product vision
- A replacement LMS
- A generic AI training platform
